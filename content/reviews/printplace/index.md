## Material Evaluation: 20pt Composite vs. True 30mil PVC

The primary limitation of PrintPlace's plastic business card line is material thickness. PrintPlace uses **20pt plastic stock** <a href="#ref1" class="text-blue-600 no-underline hover:underline"><sup>[1]</sup></a>. 

In commercial plastic printing, 20pt translates to approximately **0.020 inches or 0.50mm**. By comparison, standard bank credit cards and luxury business credentials adhere to the **ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 standard**, requiring a **30mil (0.76mm / 760 micron)** thickness core.

*   **Wallet Flex & Warping:** 20pt plastic feels noticeably thin and flexible in hand. When stored inside a tight leather wallet, 20pt cards tend to retain curve memory and bow under body heat.
*   **Reduced Snap Rigidity:** Lacking the dense PVC core of a 30mil card, 20pt plastic flexes easily without providing a premium, rigid snap upon impact.
*   **Edge Roughness:** Automated gang-run die cutting on lighter composite stock often leaves micro-burrs along the rounded corners.

## Prepress & Design Pipeline: DIY Upload Risks

PrintPlace operates an automated commercial printing queue. While this works well for standard paper brochures, plastic printing—especially clear and frosted finishes—requires specialized prepress engineering to separate opaque text from transparent backgrounds.

Commercial inks are translucent by nature. When printed directly onto clear or frosted plastic without an underlying **opaque white ink base channel (HPI White)** <a href="#ref2" class="text-blue-600 no-underline hover:underline"><sup>[2]</sup></a>, text and logos appear washed out, ghosted, or completely unreadable when held against dark surfaces.

### PrintPlace Prepress Limitations:
*   **No Human Concierge Support:** PrintPlace relies on an automated online proofing widget. Non-technical users must independently configure their vector art and spot white layers.
*   **No Free AI-to-Vector Conversion:** If you upload a flat raster image (PNG, JPEG, or AI-generated mockup), the automated system cannot vectorize line paths, resulting in fuzzy, pixelated text.
*   **Basic Flat PDF Proofs:** Proofing is restricted to automated flat screen previews that fail to demonstrate how ambient lighting reflects across clear or frosted plastic.
