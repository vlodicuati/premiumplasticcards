## The Origin: Metal Markups in a Plastic World

To objectively review MyPlasticBusinessCard.com (MPBC), you have to understand their origin story. They are an offshoot of MyMetalBusinessCard, a company that built a massive brand selling extremely expensive, heavy stainless-steel cards to realtors and executives. 

When they expanded into plastic, they brought their aggressive pricing model with them. The core issue for buyers in 2026 is that **you are paying for the brand's marketing overhead, not a superior plastic material.** 

In our pricing audits, a standard order of 30mil clear plastic cards from MPBC can cost nearly **double** what a dedicated, factory-direct plastics manufacturer charges for the exact same ISO-standard material.

## Area 1: The Design Fee Penalty

Designing for transparent or frosted plastic is highly complex. You cannot simply upload a standard JPEG; the printer must properly map out "white underbase ink" to ensure your colors are opaque and legible on a see-through surface <sup>[3]</sup>.

**MPBC's Approach:** 
MyPlasticBusinessCard penalizes you for this complexity. If you do not possess the skills to provide perfectly layered, print-ready vector files, they charge a **mandatory $60 Design Service fee** just to set up your cards for printing <sup>[2]</sup>. 

**The Luxor Standard:**
<a href="https://luxorplasticcards.com/designs/?utm_source=premiumplasticcards&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=mpbc_review_design" class="text-blue-600 hover:text-blue-800 font-semibold underline">Luxor Plastic Cards</a> operates on a concierge model without the hidden fees. Whether you provide a perfect vector file or a napkin sketch, Luxor assigns a dedicated human design concierge to your order. They will manually configure the complex white-ink masking layers and send you a completely **free digital vector proof** for approval before printing. You are never charged a design setup fee.

## Area 2: Optical Clarity and "Haze"

When you pay a massive premium for a <a href="https://luxorplasticcards.com/products/clear-plastic-cards/?utm_source=premiumplasticcards&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=mpbc_review_clear" class="text-blue-600 hover:text-blue-800 font-semibold underline">Clear Plastic Business Card</a>, you expect it to look like polished glass. 

**MPBC's Flaw:**
Despite the high price tag, buyer complaints and material audits frequently highlight that MPBC's clear plastics suffer from "haze." Instead of true transparency, the cards exhibit a milky, cloudy film and micro-scratches from the manufacturing process. Furthermore, users frequently report that their CMYK colors look muted or "washed out" because the white underbase ink is not applied with high enough density.

**The Luxor Standard:**
Luxor exclusively uses **100% Optical-Grade 30mil PVC**. Their thermal lamination process ensures maximum light transmittance, eliminating the milky haze found in outsourced plastics. Luxor’s precision UV presses lay down a heavy, vibrant white underbase, ensuring your brand colors pop brilliantly against the clear background.
