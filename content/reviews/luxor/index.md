## 100% Virgin 30mil Optical PVC Substrate

Discount plastic printers cut production costs by blending recycled chalky fillers into their plastic or using flexible 20mil composite sheets that warp in warm environments. 

Luxor exclusively manufactures cards using **30mil (0.76mm / 760 microns) virgin optical-grade PVC** <a href="#ref1" class="text-blue-600 no-underline hover:underline"><sup>[1]</sup></a>. This provides the exact weight, structural rigidity, and satisfying heavy snap of a bank credit card conforming to **ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 standards**.

*   **Crystal Clear PVC:** Manufactured in dust-controlled cleanrooms, providing glass-like optical clarity (< 0.8% haze) with zero milky haze or surface pitting.
*   **Frosted Translucent PVC:** Features an engineered satin overlay that diffuses ambient light while repelling daily wallet friction, scuffs, and fingerprints.
*   **Solid Opaque PVC:** A dense, 100% light-blocking white core formulated for crisp double-sided CMYK printing without optical bleed or show-through.

Furthermore, Luxor understands that brand perfection takes refinement. Their design concierge works directly with you through **multiple design iterations**, adjusting layout elements, typography sizes, and transparency masks until you are 100% confident in the final design before it ever touches the press.

## Micro-Precision Press Tolerances

Plastic is a non-porous substrate, meaning standard commercial presses suffer from ink bleed, blurry edges, and loss of detail on fine text. 

Luxor operates the **most precise UV lithography manufacturing process in the plastics industry**. By combining specialized state-of-the-art UV-cure presses with micro-registered plate alignment, Luxor can execute fine details that other card manufacturers simply blur out:

*   **Micro-Text & Ultra-Fine Lines:** Renders razor-sharp text as small as **3.5pt font** and thin vector lines down to **0.25pt width** without ink splatter or line breakup.
*   **Subtle Highlights & Color Gradients:** Accurately reproduces delicate color highlights, soft drop shadows, and complex photographic gradients that maintain rich depth across clear, frosted, or solid PVC.
*   **Perfect White Mask Registration:** Pinpoint alignment between the CMYK color layer and the underlying **HPI White ink base** <a href="#ref2" class="text-blue-600 no-underline hover:underline"><sup>[2]</sup></a>, eliminating ugly white halo edges around text and logos.
