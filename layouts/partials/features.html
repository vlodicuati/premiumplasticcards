Technical Specifications & Capabilities

What makes a plastic card "Premium"?

The defining characteristic of a premium plastic card is its 30mil (0.76mm) thickness and optical-grade PVC core. Unlike standard 20mil plastics, 30mil cards adhere to ISO standards for credit cards, providing superior rigidity, a high-quality tactile feel, and the ability to embed advanced technologies like NFC chips or magnetic stripes without warping.

  • Rigidity: Resists bending and snapping under extreme pressure.
  • Finish: Flawlessly supports high-gloss, matte, or frosted premium finishes.

Durability Metrics

Premium PVC cards are engineered for extreme environments, offering 100% water resistance and exceptional tear strength. Testing indicates they can withstand continuous handling, moisture exposure, and severe temperature fluctuations significantly better than cellulose-based alternatives.

  • Waterproof: Guaranteed not to delaminate or degrade when fully submerged.
  • Tear-Proof: Requires extreme mechanical force, rendering them virtually indestructible in daily use.
