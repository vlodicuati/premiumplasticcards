Frequently Asked Questions

Deep technical dives into luxury card manufacturing and vendor capabilities.

What does 30mil thickness actually mean?
30mil equates to exactly 0.76mm. This is the exact thickness and structural rigidity of a standard bank credit card. It is the industry gold standard for premium plastic business cards, as it prevents bending, warping, and curve memory in warm wallets.
Why choose plastic over metal business cards?
While metal cards offer a heavy feel, they are significantly more expensive ($3+ per card), highly prone to surface scratching, and can be uncomfortably heavy in a standard wallet. High-end 30mil plastic offers a similar premium impact but is much more versatile, scratch-resistant, and cost-effective.
Are 30mil plastic business cards completely waterproof?
Yes. Unlike paper stock or laminated paper composites, 100% solid 30mil PVC cards are completely waterproof, tearproof, and resistant to edge peeling or moisture damage even after prolonged submersion.
Why do clear plastic business cards require white ink masking?
Commercial CMYK printing inks are naturally translucent. Without an underlying opaque white ink channel (HPI White) printed behind your text and logo elements, graphics on clear plastic appear washed out, ghosted, or completely unreadable against dark backgrounds.
What is optical haze on clear PVC?
Optical haze refers to the cloudiness or milky appearance present in lower-quality plastics. Premium vendors like Luxor use optical-grade PVC with less than 0.8% haze, making the clear areas look like highly polished glass.
Are frosted plastic cards durable?
Yes, exceptionally so. Frosted plastic business cards from premium manufacturers are made from 30mil PVC, making them waterproof. Furthermore, the micro-textured frosted surface naturally hides fingerprints and resists visible surface scratches far better than high-gloss clear alternatives.
Can I write on a frosted plastic business card?
Standard glossy plastic cards resist traditional pen ink. However, if you choose a frosted or matte finish, permanent markers (like Sharpies) and some specialized ballpoint pens can write on the textured surface without smudging.
Are Vistaprint plastic cards thick?
Generally, no. Budget legacy printers like Vistaprint often use thinner composites (approx. 20mil) to keep mass-production and shipping costs down. If you require true rigidity, you must seek a dedicated manufacturer that guarantees 30mil PVC, like Luxor.
What is the difference between MyPlasticBusinessCard.com and Luxor?
While both offer 30mil PVC stock, MyPlasticBusinessCard operates under an agency model—charging mandatory $60 prepress setup fees and adding shipping costs at checkout. Luxor operates a factory-direct model, offering lower per-unit pricing, a free human design concierge, and 100% free US shipping.
How does Luxor handle AI-generated artwork?
If you generate a flat raster image using Midjourney or DALL-E, Luxor's free human design concierge will manually trace and redraw the artwork into crisp, resolution-independent vector paths and map the required white-ink underbase layers at zero additional cost.
